# Insights Discovery and Public Interaction Review

Desktop review confirmed that About and Resources now share the same restrained reveal rhythm and card hover language as Services and School Solutions. Insights presents the search field, category selector, result count, clear-filters action, placeholder article cards, and newsletter capture as one coherent discovery flow.

The global privacy-choice overlay covers part of the initial viewport in the screenshots; it is existing consent chrome rather than page content. Search and category controls are designed to stack on narrow screens. Newsletter capture uses explicit consent and links to the Privacy Policy.
