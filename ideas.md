## Selected approach: Executive Observatory

### Design Movement

Contemporary editorial modernism with the composure of a premium strategy consultancy and the clarity of a well-designed learning journal.

### Core Principles

1. Use deep navy as a field of trust, with precise gold accents as signals of insight rather than decoration.
2. Create hierarchy through asymmetry, strong editorial typography, and generous negative space.
3. Make transformation feel tangible with diagrams, directional motifs, and proof-oriented content.
4. Keep interactions restrained, tactile, and purposeful: every hover or reveal should clarify the next step.

### Color Philosophy

The palette is built on midnight navy, ink blue, warm ivory, and antique gold. Navy communicates steadiness and authority; gold marks the moments where understanding becomes action; ivory gives the page room to breathe and keeps the experience human rather than corporate-cold.

### Layout Paradigm

A split editorial composition: a wide narrative column paired with a narrow signal rail for labels, stats, and directional notes. Sections should alternate between dark and ivory fields, using anchored cards and offset blocks instead of a repetitive centered grid.

### Signature Elements

- A fine gold trajectory line that appears as a route marker through the page.
- Small uppercase index labels such as 01 / ORIENTATION and 03 / APPLICATION.
- Soft grain, hairline borders, and inset dark panels that make the page feel tactile and considered.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like opening a well-made field guide. Links underline or shift with a gold marker, cards lift slightly and reveal a directional arrow, and mobile navigation remains simple and obvious.

### Animation

Use short, 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, links, and cards. On first view, fade and translate editorial blocks upward in staggered groups. Animate the gold trajectory line with a subtle draw-on effect only when reduced motion is not requested. Never use bouncing, looping, or decorative movement that competes with the message.

### Typography System

Use Fraunces for display headlines and section statements, with Manrope for body copy, navigation, labels, and CTAs. Headlines are sentence case with tight leading and occasional italic emphasis. Labels are 11–12px uppercase with generous tracking. Body copy stays between 16–18px with relaxed line height.

### Brand Essence

Profound IQ Consulting turns insight into professional momentum for learners, leaders, and organizations navigating change. Personality: discerning, catalytic, grounded.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are clear, confident, and slightly provocative. CTAs are direct invitations to move from reflection to action. Microcopy is specific and reassuring, never inflated.

Example lines:

- “Clarity is a capability.”
- “Build the next version of how you lead.”

### Wordmark & Logo

Use a compact symbol formed from two intersecting paths: one vertical stem for depth of thought and one rising diagonal for applied growth. Pair it with a custom-feeling wordmark treatment: PROFOUND IQ in compact uppercase with CONSULTING set beneath in small tracked letters.

### Signature Brand Color

Antique Gold — #C9A34E. It should appear sparingly, as a visual signal for insight, progress, and high-value moments.

## Style Decisions

- Use a dark hero with ivory reading surfaces for contrast and pacing.
- Keep gold reserved for focus, action, and directional motifs.
- Avoid generic stock-consultancy visuals; favor a custom generated abstract editorial hero artwork.

## Accepted review amendments

- Antique Gold #C9A34E remains a signal color only; the contact area now uses midnight navy with an ivory inquiry panel, reserving gold for labels, route lines, emphasis, and the primary action.
- The trajectory motif now recurs as route-marker lines and nodes in the offerings, proof, and contact sections.
- The method visual now uses the custom abstract pathway artwork instead of a generic executive portrait.
- The proof section is framed as measured evidence with a lived-impact label and stronger editorial hierarchy.
